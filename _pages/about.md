Welcome! I am an Associate Professor of Economics at [Concordia University](https://www.concordia.ca/) in Montreal. My research studies household heterogeneity and household finance, and their interactions with the aggregate economy and macroeconomic policies.

I am a [CIRANO](https://www.cirano.qc.ca/) Researcher and a [CIREQ](https://www.cireqmontreal.com/) Research Fellow. I serve as an Associate Editor at the *Journal of Economic Theory and Econometrics*.
