I am an Associate Professor of Economics at [Concordia University](https://www.concordia.ca/) in Montreal. My research studies **business cycles**, **household finance**, and **wealth inequality**, using quantitative macroeconomic models.

I am a [CIRANO](https://www.cirano.qc.ca/) Researcher and a [CIREQ](https://www.cireqmontreal.com/) Research Fellow.

## Recent Updates

- **[2026]** "Sources of Rising Student Debt in the US" (with Jung Hwan Kim) forthcoming in *Review of Economic Dynamics*
- **[2025]** New working paper: "Monetary Policy, Wealth Inequality, and Lifecycle Dynamics" (with C. Bustamante and E. Ma)
- **[2025]** New working paper: "Stimulus Effects of Student Debt Relief Program" (with M. Irwin)
